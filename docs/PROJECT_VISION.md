# Student OS — Project Vision

## Overview

Student OS is a free, all-in-one student platform designed to support productivity, preparation, achievement, planning and milestones for students.

The name reflects the goal of acting as a single operating system for a student's academic life — a central place where planning, preparation, tracking and reflection come together.

## Mission

Give every student a free, unified, and adaptable tool to organize their academic journey, prepare for their goals, and celebrate milestones — regardless of which educational path or examination they follow.

## What It Is

Student OS is an all-in-one platform covering:

- **Productivity** — daily organization, tasks, focus and scheduling.
- **Preparation** — structured study and preparation workflows.
- **Achievement** — a record of accomplishments, skills and progress.
- **Planning** — short-term and long-term academic planning.
- **Milestones** — tracking and celebrating significant goals.

## Core Principles

### Exam-agnostic by design
The platform must support students from different educational paths and examinations without making any single exam the core purpose. Students may follow school curricula, national exams, international exams, university admissions, vocational paths, or self-directed learning. No one path is privileged.

### Free and accessible
Student OS is and will remain free, relying on free or open-source solutions. Education should not be gated behind paid subscriptions.

### Student-first
Every feature should serve a real student need and stay simple, intuitive and non-distracting.

## Guiding Values

1. **Adaptability** — Features work across many curricula and exam systems.
2. **Simplicity** — Avoid clutter; each feature has a clear purpose.
3. **Privacy** — Students control and own their data; minimal data collection.
4. **Momentum** — Help students see progress, not just tasks.
5. **Sustainability** — Built on free/open-source technology so it can stay free.

## Non-Goals (for now)

- No single-exam-specific branding or lock-in.
- No paid tiers or premium features.
- No monetization strategies that compromise access.
